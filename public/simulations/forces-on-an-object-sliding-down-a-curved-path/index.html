Force Analysis Legend

● Weight (mg): Always points straight down
● mg sin θ: Component parallel to slope (causes acceleration)
● mg cos θ: Component perpendicular to slope
● Normal Force: Balances mg cos θ in this model because the acceleration is taken to be tangent to the slope

Model assumption: At every point, the acceleration is taken to be tangent to the local slope. Therefore the normal component of acceleration is zero, so the normal force balances mg cos θ.

Key insight: Watch how the green arrow (mg sin θ) gets shorter as the slope angle decreases, showing why the tangential acceleration decreases even though the speed increases.
