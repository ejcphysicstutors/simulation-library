Think about it

What do you think will happen if the gold leaf electroscope is charged positively instead, and UV light is shone on the zinc plate?

Positively charged
Gold leaf rises and spreads apart
The electroscope now lacks electrons — the positive charge on the plate, stem and leaves causes electrostatic repulsion and the leaves spread apart.
No effect from UV light
Leaf stays raised even under UV light
UV light still ejects electrons from the zinc plate — but since the electroscope is positively charged, it attracts those electrons back. The positive charge is not removed, so the leaf remains spread.
