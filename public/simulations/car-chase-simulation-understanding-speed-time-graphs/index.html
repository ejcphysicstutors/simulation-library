Key Physics Concepts

The Area Under the Speed-Time Graph = Distance Traveled

Each car's position equals the area under its speed-time curve from t=0 to current time.

Finding Critical Points:

1. Maximum Separation (t = 12s, Distance = 90m):

  • Occurs when both cars have the same speed (both lines intersect at 40 m/s)
  • Car A has traveled 480m, Car B has traveled 390m
  • After this point, Car B is faster, so they start getting closer

2. Cars Meet Again (t = 25s, Distance = 0m):

  • Occurs when areas under both curves are equal
  • Both cars have traveled exactly 1000m
  • Despite different speed patterns, total distances are identical

Motion Details:

  • Car A (Red): Constant speed of 40 m/s throughout
  • Car B (Blue): Starts at 25 m/s, accelerates at 1.25 m/s² until t=20s, then constant 50 m/s
  • Key insight: Car B starts slower but accelerates, eventually overtaking Car A

Interactive Features:

  • Drag the green line on the speed-time graph to explore any moment
  • Use the time slider for precise control
  • Watch for visual highlights: Orange glow at maximum separation, green glow when cars meet
  • Compare areas: See how equal areas under different curves lead to same distances
