Tidal locking condition 1

Charon's rotation period = Charon's orbital period

Charon always shows the same face to Pluto. Watch the cyan dot on Charon — it always points toward Pluto.

Tidal locking condition 2

Charon's orbital period = Pluto's rotation period

Pluto and Charon are mutually tidally locked. The red dot on Pluto always faces toward Charon.

Real data

Orbital period: 6.387 Earth days • Distance: ~19,570 km • Charon is unusually large (mass ratio ~1:8) • They orbit a common barycenter.

What this means

From Pluto's surface, Charon hangs motionless in the sky. From Charon, Pluto is similarly stationary. This mutual locking is unique in our solar system.
