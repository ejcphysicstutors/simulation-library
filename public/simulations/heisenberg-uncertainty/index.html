Momentum distribution — single wavelength (p₀ only)
Δp ≈ 0 — only one wavelength is present. The wavefunction is a pure sinusoid extending across all space. Δx → ∞: the particle could be anywhere. This illustrates the limiting behaviour described by Δx · Δp ≈ h: as Δp tends towards zero, Δx becomes arbitrarily large.
