Neutral

Scientific Context:

Light can eject electrons when the photon energy E = hf exceeds the metal's work function (Φ). If the plate is Neutral, it will become Positively Charged (blue circles) as electrons leave. If it is already Negative (red circles), it will lose its charge and the leaf will fall.
